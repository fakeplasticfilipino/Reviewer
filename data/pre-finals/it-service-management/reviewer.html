1 · Definitions

The exam gives the description and asks for the term, or the other way around. Learn the key words.

TermDefinition
IT Services marketA range of services organizations use to create, manage, and deliver information, including services that assist other business functions.
EconomyThe structure of production, distribution, and utilization of goods and services. This includes IT services that keep IT infrastructure, applications, and systems efficiently implemented, operated, and optimized.
IT Consulting and ImplementationAdvisory services for businesses to evaluate their technology strategies and align them with their business strategies or internal processes.
Business Process Outsourcing (BPO)The transfer of the entire business process from a company to an IT-based third party.
IT Outsourcing (ITO)The external contracting of IT functions, services, and projects instead of relying on company-owned resources.
Other IT ServicesEverything not covered by the other three markets: system integration, software installation and support, IT education and training.
ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library)An ITSM framework used to provide guidance, not to prepare an organization for an ITIL inspection.
Supported serviceThe IT department's resources are deployed to meet the customer's demands.
Delivered serviceThe IT department produces a product or report that fulfills the needs of a customer.
Change ManagementUnder Service Transition. Implements new IT services while lessening the disruption to existing services.
Incident ManagementUnder Service Operation. Analyzes incidents and escalation steps, and restores services when an interruption happens.
Blackout planThe plan a Service Manager keeps ready in case a change implementation begins to compromise existing services.
Culture (in an organization)The practices, attitudes, behaviors, beliefs, and values shared between its members.
Service cultureThe positive consideration of the importance of understanding good service and the appreciation for giving good service to internal and external customers.
BPO vs ITO trap: BPO hands over a whole business process (customer service, HR). ITO contracts out IT functions, services, and projects (developers, network management).

Front office

Back office

Internal work, e.g. human resources

Service Manager's questions: customer vs IT

Customer's expectations (3)

• What is the expected service, and how is it delivered?
• How is the service used?
• How much does the service cost?
Purpose: to at least understand the customer experience.

IT department's capability (2)

• How is the service supported?
• How is the service delivered?

Tip: if the question mentions cost or use, it's a customer question. If it says supported or delivered, it's an IT capability question. ("How is it delivered" appears in both lists, but the IT one is only about delivery.)

Supported vs delivered

Supported

Resources deployed to meet demand. Example: Finisar, where IT provided enough server space for the customer support team.

Delivered

A product or report is produced that fulfills the customer's needs.

Internal vs external customer (delivered products)

Internal customer

An essential financial report derived from several databases, built into Oracle

External customer

A customizable web page design palette that anyone can use

Service Operation

Incident Management: restore ASAP, isolate the cause, prevent recurrence

The 4 influences of service culture

Influence on…Main idea · example
1. How an organization is seenA brand is tested by how customers are treated across the whole experience (website → ordering → after-sales). A consistent message builds the brand. · Shopee
2. Operation's effectivenessCulture reflects core values. Problems arise when culture doesn't align with vision and strategy, or the imposed culture differs from reality. · Scam hubs exposé (Sen. Risa Hontiveros)
3. How employees feel about their jobsTurns employees into advocates, not critics. Recognizing excellent work makes them feel it matters, and helps retain the best employees and attract the best recruits. · Lazada
4. How the operation learns and adaptsCompare what is happening with what should be happening, which needs a clear idea of how service should be delivered. · IBM Philippines
Memory hook: "Seen · Effective · Feel · Adapt" → the brand outside, the operations inside, the people, the future.

3 · Who does what

Each responsibility belongs to exactly one role. The exam will try to swap them.

RoleResponsibilities
Service Manager
"the boss", executive role		 • Leads a service team
• Defines specific services and how they're delivered, in line with business expectations
• Decides how ITIL guidance is best integrated into the organization
• Answers the 3 customer and 2 IT-capability questions
• Change Management: keeps a blackout plan ready, assesses the risk of any change, communicates the change to relevant groups
• Incident Management: restores the service ASAP, isolates the cause, prevents recurrence
• Can come from outside IT if they have technical literacy and working knowledge of the organization's IT capabilities
• The position can be rotated to keep new ideas flowing
Service Team
"a kind of board director"		 • Oversees advice and execution during a service change
• Primary duty: keep the Service Manager well-informed (accountability, tracking metrics)
• Ensures the service stays effective after implementation
• First to suggest improvements, based on tracking metrics
• Long-term planning and anticipating how technology and business evolve
• Made up of tech experts, product experts, support personnel, and executive leadership
Service Sponsor
executive leadership in the Service Team		 • Usually someone directly acquainted with the business side
• Sets the budget for services
• Qualifies associated risks
• Sets acceptable service levels
Service Manager + Service TeamSpearhead the company's ITSM efforts together, so regular meetings and consultations are a must.
Common swap: the budget is set by the Service Sponsor, not the Service Manager. Keeping the manager informed is the Service Team's job.
